Company Map

Customer demand → Planning

Planning converts demand and inventory information into production requirements.

Planning → Production → Warehouse

Production consumes materials; Warehouse holds and receives physical stock.

Requirement → Procurement → Supplier

Procurement validates the requirement, follows governance, manages supplier/commercial work and creates the buying path.

Supplier → Warehouse → Finance

Goods arrive, receipts are recorded, invoices are matched and exceptions are investigated.
