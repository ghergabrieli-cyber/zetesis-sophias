# Zetesis Sophias — Simulation Contract

## Canonical learning loop
Situation → Investigation → Action → Consequence → Reflection/Debrief → Transfer → Mastery.

## Truth ownership
Formal simulation state owns facts: business data, policies, contracts, supplier state, financial data, constraints and consequences. AI characters may express those facts but must not invent canonical state.

## Consequences
Choices do not resolve into simple correct/incorrect markers when reality supports trade-offs. Decisions update world state, stakeholder reactions, risks, recovery work and future information.

## Scaffolding
Early missions tell the learner what to inspect. Scaffolding then fades. Advanced missions may contain hidden problems, harmless anomalies and "nothing is wrong" cases.

## Evidence
The platform records investigation path, artifacts inspected, questions asked, decisions, reasoning, recovery and transfer — not merely final answers.

## Responsibility
Progression is access-based:
Observer → Transactional → Supplier → Commercial → Sourcing → Portfolio → Category → Strategic → Executive.

"Hero" means the learner can enter an unfamiliar professional situation and form a defensible path forward without being helpless.
