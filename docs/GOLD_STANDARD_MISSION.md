# Zetesis Sophias — Gold-Standard Mission Template

Every flagship mission should use this structure unless the professional reality requires a justified variation.

1. **Situation** — a business event, not an abstract lesson.
2. **Investigation** — multiple sources/artifacts with incomplete information.
3. **Working model** — learner must connect facts, not merely collect them.
4. **Decision** — several plausible actions where possible.
5. **Consequence** — choice changes formal world state; no arbitrary red X.
6. **Recovery** — poor decisions remain educational and recoverable when reality permits.
7. **Debrief** — separates facts, judgment, uncertainty and causal effects.
8. **Shadow timeline** — shows counterfactual outcomes without pretending there was only one thought process.
9. **Transfer** — learner states or applies the principle in a different context.
10. **Mastery evidence** — KNOW / DO / JUDGE / REPEAT / TRANSFER, accumulated across missions.

## Absolute-zero rule
No professional term may be required before it is introduced in context. Vocabulary is taught at the moment it becomes useful.

## Professional uncertainty rule
“I don’t know yet” is valid only when paired with a precise statement of what information is missing and why it changes the decision.

## AI boundary
Scenario truth lives in structured state. AI characters may communicate truth, ambiguity, opinion or stakeholder pressure, but may not silently invent canonical facts.
